Subject: Advanced RTU Control Retrofit with Measured Savings

1. SUMMARY

This proposal covers an advanced control retrofit for the eight rooftop units serving Eastgate Building 2. We project a 12% reduction in HVAC electricity use, measured against a weather-normalized baseline and verified after installation.

2. BASELINE

The baseline is the HVAC electricity consumption of Building 2 for the 12 months from July 2025 through June 2026, taken from the eight RTU submeters installed in 2023. Baseline HVAC consumption was 742,000 kWh. Whole-building consumption for the same period was 1,610,000 kWh; savings figures in this proposal apply to HVAC consumption only.

3. EXISTING CONDITIONS

- Eight (8) packaged RTUs, 12.5 to 20 tons, installed 2014
- BAS with occupancy schedules and night setback
- Constant-speed supply fans; fans run continuously during occupied hours
- Economizers functional on all units (verified by damper test on August 21, 2026)
- Preventive maintenance performed quarterly; last coil cleaning April 2026

4. PROPOSED SOLUTION

- Advanced RTU controller with multi-speed fan control, integrated economizer control, and demand-controlled ventilation (CO2 sensing)
- Controller power draw: 180 W total for all eight controllers and gateway combined
- BACnet/IP integration with the existing BAS

5. PROJECTED SAVINGS

We project a 12% reduction in HVAC electricity consumption, or approximately 89,000 kWh per year against the 742,000 kWh baseline.

6. MEASUREMENT AND VERIFICATION

Savings will be measured and verified using IPMVP Option B (retrofit isolation) with the existing RTU submeters. The baseline and reporting periods will be normalized for weather using cooling and heating degree days from the nearest NOAA station. A savings report will be delivered at 6 and 12 months after installation. If verified year-one savings fall below 9%, [Vendor B] will refund 25% of the installed price.

7. INVESTMENT

Total installed price: $38,500. This includes all hardware, installation, integration, commissioning, and the first year of software. Software after year one: $1,200 per year, optional.

8. SCHEDULE

Installation over 4 working days, with optimization active within 3 weeks of contract execution.

9. ASSUMPTIONS AND EXCLUSIONS

- Existing RTUs are in good working order (confirmed by April 2026 maintenance records)
- Excludes replacement of failed mechanical components
