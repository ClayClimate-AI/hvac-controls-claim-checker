Weeknight Turkey Chili (serves 6)

Ingredients:
- 2 lb ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans, drained
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:
1. Brown the turkey in a large pot over medium-high heat, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the onion and garlic and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, beans, and spices.
4. Simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Taste, adjust seasoning, and serve. Reduces fat by about 18% compared with beef chili.
